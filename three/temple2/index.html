Chapter One 山門 · Sanmon

The Threshold

山門

The torii rises from the dark like a gate of cinnabar. Beyond it, stone steps climb toward the hall, each riser worn smooth by weather and years. This is the point of crossing — where the road becomes a path, and the path becomes a vow.

Chapter Two 石庭 · Sekitei

Still Gardens

石庭

Raked gravel holds the shape of water that is not there. Stones stand in a silence so complete it becomes audible. In the dry garden, nothing moves, and yet everything is in motion — the moon shifting, the mind settling.

Chapter Three 松 · Matsu

The Pines

松

Wind moves through the black pines like a memory of the sea. Needles fall without hurry, and the air tastes of resin and cold. The mountain keeps its oldest trees close, as if afraid the night would lose them.

Chapter Four 灯籠 · Tōrō

Sacred Craft

灯籠

Stone lanterns, cut by hands that no longer have names, hold small fires against the dark. Shoji glow the color of embers and paper. Light, here, is a discipline — tended, trimmed, never wasted.

Chapter Five 残照 · Zanshō

Afterlight

残照

The moon, vermilion and immense, rises over the ridge. What the day could not say, the afterlight whispers: nothing is lost that was witnessed. You descend the mountain the way you came — and yet not the same.

影 · Afterword

A silent cinema in three dimensions.

Kage is an exercise in restraint — a place built from light, shadow, and slow attention. It has no product to sell and no call to action. It only asks that you walk, and look, and let the night do the rest.
